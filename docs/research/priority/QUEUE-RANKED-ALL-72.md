# Full queue ranked, all 72, by expected Google traffic (Claude's judgment, 2026-10-22)

**Not measured.** No keyword tool used. Each rank is my judgment from how people search for a river gauge, with the reason. Search Console will overwrite this once the live pages gather impressions. Gauge-location fixes from the 2026-10-22 review are folded in (San Marcos, lower Salt, Gauley cfs).

The reader searches "<river> water level", "<river> level", "<river> flow", "<river> cfs", "<river> at <place>". Demand comes from: tubers/floaters (huge in summer, near cities), whitewater rafters on release rivers (every trip), big-city flood watchers (steady, spikes in floods), trout anglers on tailwaters (daily, but fragmented).
