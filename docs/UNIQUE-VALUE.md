# Unique value: what each gauge page gives that the raw USGS page does not

Test for every feature: real data, one plain mechanism sentence, a source line, limits stated. A feature that cannot pass is removed, not reworded. Pass 1 of the outside review said the differentiation is unproven; this file is the list to test with real river users.

| Feature | Real data | What the reader gets | Limit stated on the page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compared with past years (dial) | USGS daily statistics, 10 to 90 years per gauge | One plain word and sentence: how today ranks against the same date in past years | estimate from the latest reading or the 24 h mean (tidal gauges use the mean); dams may have changed flows |
| Change, 24 h / 6 h / 1 h | USGS continuous readings | Direction and size, with a word that agrees with the sign | "Unclear" when flow and stage disagree; gauge below a dam |
| Flood stages and impacts ladder | NWS NWPS point linked to this USGS id | Distance to Action / Minor / Moderate / Major, impact statements | NWS point name can differ; margins use the NWS reading when zero points differ |
| NWS alerts, live | api.weather.gov in the browser | Banner and tile follow the live answer | "Unknown" when the source did not answer; never "none" on a failure |
| River forecast + hourly weather | NWPS, NWS | Forecast high, timing, storms | not issued / out of date stated; not a safety statement |
| 7-day, 30-day, earlier-years charts | USGS | Shape of the last days against the median band | gaps are real gaps |
| Upstream / nearby gauges | USGS NLDI, lag measured | What is coming from upstream | tributary note when much smaller |
| Flood record, peaks, frequency | USGS peaks, NWS stages | How often this river reached each stage | datum caveat |
| Your range | the reader's own numbers, browser only | In / below / above the flow they care about | stays on the device |
| Save gauge, CSV | USGS readings | Keep and export | last 7 days |

## Not claimed
No safe / unsafe / runnable verdict, no "best" anything, no access points or hazards (needs data we have no rights to), no forecast of our own.

## To prove or kill (needs real people, owner)
Show five river users one page on a phone: do they find the flow, its age, the alert state and the comparison in 30 seconds; do they prefer this page to USGS or American Whitewater for that task; which feature did they never notice. Write the answers here. A feature nobody uses goes.
